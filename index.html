Why YAGLA

Every household runs on the same broken system: someone shouts down the hall, someone else sends a photo of a scrap of paper, and three people forget the milk anyway. A note you screenshot isn't a shared list.

One shared list, live

Everyone in the household adds to the same list, and changes appear on every phone straight away.

Shopping Mode

The list rearranges itself into the order of the shop, aisle by aisle, so you never double back for the bread.

Tick off together

Whoever's shopping ticks items as they go, and everyone else watches the list shrink in real time.

Know who added what

Every item shows who put it on the list, so you can ask before you guess.

Works offline

Tick things off in the dead zone at the back of the shop; it syncs the moment you have signal again.
